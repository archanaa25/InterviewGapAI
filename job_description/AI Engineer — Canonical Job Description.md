# AI Engineer

## About the Role

We are looking for an AI Engineer to design, build, and deploy intelligent applications using modern AI and Generative AI technologies.

You will work on real-world AI use cases involving Large Language Models, Retrieval-Augmented Generation, AI agents, enterprise data, and external systems. You will collaborate with engineering and product teams to take AI solutions from experimentation through evaluation and production deployment.

## Key Responsibilities

- Design and develop AI and Generative AI applications using Python and modern LLM frameworks.
- Build Retrieval-Augmented Generation solutions over structured and unstructured enterprise knowledge.
- Develop AI agents and workflows capable of interacting with APIs, tools, databases, and external systems.
- Evaluate AI applications for quality, accuracy, reliability, and performance.
- Integrate AI capabilities into applications through APIs and services.
- Design scalable and maintainable AI solutions while considering security, latency, cost, and reliability.
- Deploy, monitor, troubleshoot, and continuously improve AI applications in production.
- Collaborate with product, engineering, and business teams to translate requirements into practical AI solutions.

## Required Skills

- Strong Python and software-engineering fundamentals.
- Good understanding of machine learning, Generative AI, and Large Language Models.
- Hands-on experience building LLM-powered applications.
- Experience with Retrieval-Augmented Generation, embeddings, vector search, and related retrieval techniques.
- Understanding of AI agents, tool integration, and workflow orchestration.
- Experience evaluating and improving the quality of AI applications.
- Experience integrating APIs, databases, and external systems.
- Familiarity with cloud platforms, containers, and production deployment practices.
- Strong analytical, debugging, and problem-solving skills.
- Ability to explain technical decisions and architectural trade-offs clearly.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Experience with frameworks such as LangChain, LangGraph, LlamaIndex, or similar.
- Experience with vector databases and modern search technologies.
- Familiarity with AI evaluation and observability frameworks.
- Understanding of AI security, guardrails, and responsible AI practices.
- Exposure to MLOps/LLMOps and production AI monitoring.

## Experience

Approximately 2–5 years of software, machine-learning, data, or AI engineering experience, with hands-on exposure to building AI-powered applications.

Equivalent experience demonstrated through significant projects, research, open-source contributions, or other practical work may also be considered.
